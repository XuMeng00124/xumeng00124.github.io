About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computational Mathematics at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Ya-Feng Liu. I'm also very fortunate to work with Prof. Anthony Man-Cho So at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and Prof. Bo Jiang at Nanjing Normal University. Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Computational Mathematics from the School of Mathematics, Jilin University.

My research focuses on mathematical optimization, particularly Riemannian nonsmooth and minimax optimization, and its applications in machine learning. I am currently especially interested in LLM post-training and LLM agents. I also spent the summer of 2026 at Alibaba Group, working on LLM post-training for creative writing.
